## 1. Camera Setup & Framing

SignBridge tracks 21 3D hand joint landmarks and measures their positions relative to your head, chin, and chest zones.

- **Position**: Center your upper body (head to chest) in the camera frame.
- **Distance**: Hold your hand **30–60 cm (1–2 feet)** away from the camera.
- **Hold Duration**: Hold each sign steady for **2 camera scans (~0.8 seconds)** to commit the word/number.

## 2. Numbers (0–10) Performance Guide

| Number | How to Perform the Gesture |
| :---: | :--- |
| **`0`** | Touch the tip of your **Thumb to your Index Finger** in an 'O' circle shape (👌) with other fingers tucked down. |
| **`1`** | Extend your **Index Finger straight up** (☝️) with Middle, Ring, Pinky, and Thumb folded. |
| **`2`** | Extend your **Index and Middle Fingers** straight up in a V-shape (✌️). |
| **`3`** | Extend your **Thumb, Index, and Middle Fingers** (fold Ring and Pinky). |
| **`4`** | Extend **4 Fingers** (Index, Middle, Ring, Pinky) straight up; tuck your Thumb across your palm. |
| **`5`** | Extend and spread **all 5 fingers** wide open (🖐️). |
| **`6`** | Touch your **Thumb tip to your Pinky tip**, keeping your Index, Middle, and Ring fingers straight up. |
| **`7`** | Touch your **Thumb tip to your Ring tip**, keeping your Index, Middle, and Pinky fingers straight up. |
| **`8`** | Touch your **Thumb tip to your Middle tip**, keeping your Index, Ring, and Pinky fingers straight up. |
| **`9`** | Touch your **Thumb tip to your Index tip**, keeping your Middle, Ring, and Pinky fingers straight up. |
| **`10`** | Make a **Thumbs Up (👍) and shake your hand gently side-to-side**. |

## 3. Body & Facial Position Gestures

MediaPipe tracks the height of your hand relative to your head, chin, and chest.

### A. Forehead / Temple Zone (Top of Frame)
- **`"Learn" / "Study"`**: Place your extended index finger near your **forehead/temple**.
- **`"Why"`**: Touch your forehead with fingers and pull your hand away into a Y-shape.

### B. Mouth / Lips / Chin Zone (Middle-Upper Frame)
- **`"Food" / "Eat"`**: Pinch all 5 fingertips together and hold near your **mouth/lips**.
- **`"Water"`**: Extend 3 fingers in a W-shape (Index, Middle, Ring) near your **chin/mouth**.
- **`"Who"`**: Circle your extended index finger near your **chin**.

### C. Chest & Heart Zone (Middle Frame)
- **`"Please"`**: Place an open flat palm on your **chest** and make a clockwise circle.
- **`"Sorry"`**: Make a loose fist and rub gently in a circle over your **chest**.
- **`"I Am Fine"`**: Tap your open hand thumb against your **center chest**.
- **`"Police"`**: Place a C-shaped hand over your **left chest** (like a badge).
- **`"Call Me"`**: Make a phone shape (Thumb & Pinky extended 🤙) near your **ear/face**.

## 4. Words & Phrases Guide

| Word / Phrase | How to Perform |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`"Hello"`** | Open palm facing camera, waving gently **side-to-side**. |
| **`"Thank You"`** | Flat palm starting near chin and sweeping **downward/outward**. |
| **`"Good Morning"`** | Flat palm held at chest height moving **vertically upward**. |
| **`"I Love You"`** | Extend **Thumb, Index, and Pinky** fingers (🤟). |
| **`"Yes"`** | Hold a static **Thumbs Up** (👍). |
| **`"No"`** | Hold a static **Thumbs Down** (👎). |
| **`"Victory"` / `"Peace"`** | Extend **Index and Middle** fingers in a V-sign (✌️). |
| **`"Okay"`** | Touch **Thumb to Index** in a circle, keep other 3 fingers up (👌). |
| **`"Stop"` / `"Wait"`** | Hold a flat, open palm **firm and still** facing the camera. |
| **`"Cool"` / `"Rock"`** | Extend **Index and Pinky** fingers (🤘). |
| **`"Point"`** | Extend **Index Finger** pointing forward (☝️). |

## 5. Tips for Maximum Accuracy

1. **Upper Body Visibility**: Make sure your head, shoulders, and chest are visible so the camera can detect body height zones.
2. **Distinct Finger Positions**: Keep un-extended fingers folded clearly against your palm.
3. **Lighting**: Bright, even lighting helps the AI track hand joints instantly.
