# ✋ How to Perform Sign Language Gestures in SignBridge

This guide explains how to use your hand and upper body to communicate with the **SignBridge** camera translator and voice studio.

---

## 📷 Camera & Positioning Basics

For maximum recognition accuracy when using **Sign → English Live Translation**:

1. **Framing**: Position your upper body (head, shoulders, and chest) so that it is clearly visible in the camera frame.
2. **Distance**: Hold your hand **30–60 cm (1 to 2 feet)** away from the phone camera.
3. **Lighting**: Ensure your hand is evenly lit without dark shadows.
4. **Hold Gesture**: Hold each sign gesture steady for **2 camera scans (~0.8 seconds)**. The status banner will display `"Detected: [Word] (Stable: 2/2)"` and automatically append the word to your sentence!

---

## 🖐️ Gesture Instructions Guide

### 1. Greetings & Courtesy
- **`Hello`**: Open your hand with all 5 fingers spread out, facing the camera, and wave side-to-side.
- **`Thank You`**: Start with a flat open palm near your chin/chest and sweep your hand downward and outward toward the camera.
- **`Good Morning`**: Place your open flat palm at chest height and move your hand vertically upward.
- **`Good Afternoon`**: Hold your open palm at eye level and move it forward and slightly downward.
- **`Welcome`**: Hold your open palm facing slightly sideways and sweep your hand gently toward your center chest.
- **`I Love You`**: Extend your **Thumb**, **Index Finger**, and **Pinky Finger** (🤟), folding down your middle two fingers.

---

### 2. Emergency & Assistance
- **`Help`**: Form a fist with your thumb up, place it on top of a flat open palm, and move both hands upward together.
- **`Water`**: Extend your **Index, Middle, and Ring fingers** in a W-shape (Pinky and Thumb tucked), and tap near your mouth/chin.
- **`Food / Eat`**: Pinch all 5 fingertips together and tap near your mouth.
- **`Stop / Wait`**: Hold an open flat palm firm and still directly facing the camera (🖐️).
- **`Call Me`**: Make a phone shape with your **Thumb and Pinky extended** (🤙) held near your ear.
- **`Police`**: Form a C-shape hand and place it over your left chest (like a badge).

---

### 3. Responses & Common Expressions
- **`Yes`**: Make a fist and extend your **Thumb pointing straight up** (Thumbs Up 👍).
- **`No`**: Make a fist and extend your **Thumb pointing straight down** (Thumbs Down 👎).
- **`Victory / Peace`**: Extend your **Index and Middle fingers** in a V-shape (✌️).
- **`Okay / Perfect`**: Touch your **Thumb tip to your Index tip** to form a circle (👌), keeping your other 3 fingers pointing up.
- **`Cool / Rock`**: Extend your **Index and Pinky fingers** (🤘), folding down your middle two fingers.
- **`Point / Look`**: Extend your **Index Finger** pointing forward (☝️).
- **`Learn / Study`**: Place your extended index finger near your forehead.

---

## 🎙️ Using the English → Sign Animation Studio

1. Navigate to **English → Sign** from the home screen.
2. **Text Input**: Type any phrase (e.g. *"Hello"*, *"Good Morning"*, *"Thank You"*, *"What Is Your Name"*) and tap **Translate to ISL Sign**.
3. **Voice Input**: Tap the **Microphone** button, speak your sentence clearly, and tap **Stop**.
4. **Voice Output (Text-To-Speech)**: Tap the **Speaker icon (🔊)** next to the translated text to hear the sentence spoken aloud in English!

---

## 💡 Quick Troubleshooting

- **Server Disconnected?**: The app features **On-Device Offline Mode**. If the server is offline, the app will automatically predict common phrases locally on your device without showing any connection errors!
- **Camera Permission**: Ensure camera permission is granted when prompted on first launch.
